Lifecycle

The four stages of continuous exposure management

SentinelASM runs this loop continuously, not just at scan time — so your inventory reflects reality, not last quarter's audit.

01 · DISCOVER

Map the footprint

Passive and active recon builds a live inventory of domains, IPs, ports and cloud assets.

02 · ASSESS

Fingerprint & scan

Every asset is fingerprinted for technology, version and known vulnerabilities.

03 · PRIORITIZE

Score with AI

Findings are ranked by exploitability, exposure and business context — not raw CVSS alone.

04 · REMEDIATE

Fix & verify

Assign, track and re-verify fixes, with a full history of every exposure window.
